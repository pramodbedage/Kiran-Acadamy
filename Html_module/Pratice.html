About Rohit Sharma

Rohit Gurunath Sharma is an Indian international cricketer and the current captain of the Indian cricket team in ODIs and Tests. He was born on 30 April 1987 in Nagpur, Maharashtra. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest opening batsmen in limited-overs cricket history. He is famous worldwide for his effortless and elegant pull shot.

World Records & Key Achievements

1. Highest Individual Score in ODI History: Rohit scored an unbelievable 264 runs off 173 balls against Sri Lanka at Eden Gardens.

2. Three ODI Double Centuries: He is the only batsman in world cricket history to hit 3 double centuries in One Day Internationals (209, 264, and 208*).

3. Most Sixes in International Cricket: He holds the world record for hitting the most sixes across all formats of international cricket (600+ sixes).

4. ICC T20 World Cup 2024 Champion: Under his fearless leadership, India won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2024 unbeaten!

5. World Cup 2019 Record: He scored 5 centuries in a single ICC Cricket World Cup edition, which is an all-time record.
